# Koinonia — Child Safety Standards

**Effective date:** May 30, 2026
**Last updated:** May 30, 2026

Koinonia is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. This page sets out the standards we uphold, how we prevent CSAE-related content and behaviour, how users can report concerns, and how we respond.

These standards apply to all users of the Koinonia mobile application and any associated services.

---

## 1. Zero-tolerance commitment

Koinonia has a zero-tolerance policy for any content, behaviour, or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, or otherwise harms children. This includes but is not limited to:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
- Sexualised content involving minors
- Grooming or any attempt to develop inappropriate relationships with minors
- Sharing personal information that could be used to identify or locate a child without consent
- Content that promotes, normalises, glorifies, or facilitates child exploitation
- Any links, references, or invitations to content, communities, or platforms that engage in CSAE

Any user found violating these standards will have their account terminated immediately, all related content removed, and the incident reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Ghanaian law enforcement where applicable.

---

## 2. Age requirements

Koinonia is intended for users aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will:

1. Immediately suspend the account
2. Delete all personal information associated with the account within 30 days
3. Where appropriate, notify the parent or guardian

Parents and guardians who believe their child under 13 has created an account should contact us immediately at **koinoniaapp.app@gmail.com**.

---

## 3. Prevention measures

Koinonia takes the following proactive measures to prevent CSAE on our platform:

- **Age-gated registration.** Users must confirm they are 13 or older to create an account.
- **Public content design.** All posts and comments on Koinonia are public by design — there is no private messaging feature. This reduces opportunities for grooming behaviour that typically occurs in private channels.
- **User reporting.** All users have access to in-app and email reporting channels (see Section 4).
- **Content review.** We review all reported content. Confirmed CSAE-related content is removed immediately, the offending account is terminated, and the incident is reported to relevant authorities.
- **Cooperation with authorities.** We cooperate fully with law enforcement and child protection agencies in investigations relating to suspected CSAE on our platform.
- **No anonymous accounts.** All Koinonia accounts require a verifiable email address. We do not permit fully anonymous registration.

---

## 4. How to report

If you encounter any content, behaviour, or account on Koinonia that you believe involves the sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of a child, please report it immediately.

### Email reporting

Send a report to **koinoniaapp.app@gmail.com** with:

- The username or display name of the account involved
- A description of the content or behaviour
- Screenshots if possible (without re-sharing harmful content)
- The approximate date and time you observed it

We will acknowledge all child-safety reports within 24 hours and investigate within 72 hours.

### Reporting to authorities

In addition to reporting to us, please also report to the appropriate authorities:

- **In the United States:** National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline at [https://report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or by phone at 1-800-843-5678
- **In Ghana:** Cyber Security Authority (CSA) at [https://www.csa.gov.gh](https://www.csa.gov.gh) or your local police service
- **In the United Kingdom:** Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) at [https://report.iwf.org.uk](https://report.iwf.org.uk)
- **In the European Union:** INHOPE network member in your country at [https://www.inhope.org](https://www.inhope.org)
- **Elsewhere:** your local law enforcement and your country's child protection agency

---

## 5. Our response process

When we receive a report or detect potential CSAE on our platform, we follow this process:

1. **Acknowledge** the report within 24 hours
2. **Investigate** by reviewing the account, content, and surrounding context within 72 hours
3. **Take action**, which may include:
   - Immediately removing the content
   - Suspending or terminating the offending account
   - Preserving evidence for law enforcement
   - Reporting the incident to NCMEC and relevant authorities
4. **Notify** the reporter of the outcome (where appropriate and lawful)

We do not negotiate with users who violate these standards. There are no warnings or second chances for confirmed CSAE violations.

## 7. Compliance with laws

Koinonia complies with all relevant child safety laws in the jurisdictions where we operate. This includes:

- The United States' Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including special protections for children
- The Ghana Data Protection Act 2012
- The United Kingdom's Age Appropriate Design Code

We report confirmed CSAM and CSAE-related incidents to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required under US law, and to Ghanaian law enforcement and child protection authorities where applicable.
